# Requirements

Capture functional behavior, quality attributes, constraints, integrations, data, security, accessibility, operations, and compatibility.

For each requirement, record source, priority, rationale, measurable acceptance criteria, and dependencies.

Challenge requirements that conflict, are not observable, or prescribe implementation without a justified constraint. Preserve unresolved decisions explicitly instead of silently choosing on behalf of the product owner.
