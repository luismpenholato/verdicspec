# Product Discovery

1. Identify the user, context, pain, and desired outcome.
2. Separate observed evidence from assumptions.
3. Define non-goals and constraints.
4. Describe the smallest end-to-end slice that can test the central assumption.
5. Define success measures and how they will be observed.
6. Record unanswered questions and propose spikes when uncertainty is technical.

## Output

Create or update `.verdicspec/product-brief.md`. Do not create implementation tasks until the problem, smallest useful slice, and success measures are explicit enough to evaluate.

Do not turn an unvalidated idea into a large technical backlog. Product discovery must precede architecture for new products unless a hard constraint requires the reverse.
