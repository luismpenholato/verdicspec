# Greenfield Product

Compose `product-discovery`, `requirements`, `technology-discovery`, `architecture`, `planning`, `testing`, `observability`, `verification`, and `release-readiness` as needed.

## Sequence

1. Validate the problem, users, outcome, and non-goals.
2. Define the smallest useful product slice.
3. Record constraints, risks, and success measures.
4. Choose architecture and technology from evidence and expected change.
5. Build a vertical slice through the real boundaries.
6. Verify behavior and operational readiness.
7. Learn from feedback before expanding the product.

Do not create speculative infrastructure or broad abstractions before the first slice proves the important assumptions.

## Outputs and gates

- Discovery: `.verdicspec/product-brief.md` approved.
- Baseline: `.verdicspec/technology-inventory.md` and `.verdicspec/system-context.md` available.
- Specification: requirements and acceptance criteria approved.
- Delivery: vertical slice verified with an explicit release decision.
